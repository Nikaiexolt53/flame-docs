This Privacy Policy describes how the Flame app collects, uses, and protects your information, as well as our commitment to user safety.

1. Child Safety & CSAE Prohibition

Flame strictly prohibits the publication, distribution, or creation of any content depicting Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Abuse Imagery (CSAE). We use both automated detection systems and manual human moderation to identify and immediately remove such content.

  • Reporting: We immediately report any detected instances of CSAE to the relevant law enforcement agencies and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  • Enforcement: Any user attempting to share such content will be subject to a permanent ban without the possibility of appeal.
