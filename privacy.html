1. Privacy Policy

This policy describes how we collect and process your personal data.

1.1. Data We Collect

  • Profile Information: Name, age, gender, email address, and phone number.
  • Media: Photos and videos uploaded for profile display and identity verification.
  • Location: Geolocation data (GPS) to match you with nearby users. This may be collected in the background if permitted.
  • Technical Data: IP address, device ID, and crash reports via Google Firebase.

1.2. How We Use Data

  • To create and manage your dating profile.
  • To facilitate matches based on distance and preferences.
  • To enable real-time video/audio communication (via Agora SDK).
  • To ensure platform safety and verify user age.

1.3. Data Retention & Deletion

We store your data as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the App Settings. Once deleted, your personal data, matches, and messages will be permanently erased from our production servers within 30 days.

2. Terms of Service

By using Flame, you agree to comply with the following rules:

2.1. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old to use this service. By creating an account, you represent and warrant that you are of legal age.

2.2. Prohibited Content & Conduct

You agree not to post content that:

  • Is illegal, hateful, or encourages violence.
  • Contains nudity, sexually explicit material, or pornography.
  • Promotes commercial services or spam.
  • Harasses or bullies other users.

2.3. Account Termination

We reserve the right to suspend or terminate any account that violates these terms or our safety standards without prior notice.

3. Child Safety & CSAE Policy

Flame is dedicated to maintaining a safe environment. We have Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse Imagery (CSAE) or Material (CSAM).

  • Monitoring: We use automated AI-based hashing technology and manual moderation to detect and remove CSAE.
  • Reporting: We comply with all legal obligations to report any instances of CSAE to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and international law enforcement.
  • Enforcement: Any user involved in the distribution of CSAE will be permanently banned and reported to the authorities immediately.
